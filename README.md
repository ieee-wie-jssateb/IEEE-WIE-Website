## 🌸 About Us

IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) is a global network dedicated to promoting women engineers and scientists and inspiring girls around the world to pursue careers in engineering and technology.

The **IEEE WIE Student Chapter at JSSATEB** strives to create an inclusive community that encourages technical excellence, innovation, leadership, and collaboration. Through technical events, workshops, and initiatives, the chapter empowers students to explore opportunities and grow in the field of engineering.

## 🌐 About the Website

This repository contains the source code for the official IEEE WIE JSSATEB Student Chapter website.

The website serves as the chapter's digital presence, showcasing its mission, initiatives, events, team, and opportunities to connect with the IEEE WIE community.

**Live Website:** https://ieee-wie-jssateb.netlify.app/

## ✨ Key Features

- **Chapter Overview** — Introduction to the chapter, its vision, and mission.
- **Events & Initiatives** — A platform to showcase chapter activities and technical events.
- **Team** — Information about the people contributing to the chapter.
- **Community Engagement** — Opportunities to connect with the IEEE WIE community.
- **Responsive Interface** — An accessible browsing experience across supported devices.
